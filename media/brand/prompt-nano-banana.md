## הפרומפט (להעתקה)

```
Use the attached poster as the exact style reference. Create a new poster
illustration in the identical visual language.

STYLE — copy precisely:
Flat vector illustration, strictly two colors on off-white paper (#fffbff):
solid black (#000000) and one saturated red (#ef1c21). No gradients, no
shadows, no texture, no 3D. Uniform-weight black outlines. Sharp corners
everywhere; circles only where intentional. Mid-century / Bauhaus poster
graphic, Swiss-modernist grid.

COMPOSITION:
Thick red border framing the whole poster, generous off-white margin inside it.
The center is a horizontal band divided into three outlined rectangular panels
of unequal width, separated by thin black rules.
- Left panel: a coupe cocktail glass (solid black bowl, stem, elliptical foot)
  with an olive pick, and a tall highball glass filled solid black with
  off-white ice cubes, topped by a red citrus wheel.
- Center panel: a bar shelf — an outlined cocktail shaker, one striped
  outlined bottle, one solid black bottle. The bottom half of the panel is
  solid black. A perfect circle badge (off-white, double black ring) straddles
  the black edge, empty inside — leave room for text.
- Right panel: concentric black arches, a solid red circle sun in the top
  corner, black stepped stairs, a thin black floor line.
Leave the top-right area and the entire lower third empty off-white — text
will be added later.

DO NOT render any text or letters anywhere in the image.
Portrait 4:5, 1080x1350.
```
